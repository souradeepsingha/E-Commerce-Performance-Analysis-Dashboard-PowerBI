# 📊 E-commerce-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard| Power BI

An interactive **E-commerce-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze sales, customers, products, orders, profitability, discounts, and delivery performance. The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful insights through interactive visualizations, KPIs, filters, and trend analysis.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to understand overall business performance and support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard provides insights into **sales performance, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, order trends, profitability, discounts, and delivery efficiency**.

The report is divided into three interactive pages:

* **E-Commerce Performance – KPIs**
* **Customer & Product Segment**
* **Order & Profit Analysis**

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🔹 1. E-Commerce Performance – KPIs

The KPI dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance using key metrics such as:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity Sold
* Total Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Average Items per Order (AIPO)
* Total Customers
* Average Revenue per Customer (ARPC)
* Sales & Quantity Trend Analysis
* Product Category & Sub-Category Filters

---

### 🔹 2. Customer & Product Segment

This page focuses on understanding customer and product performance across different regions.

Key analysis includes:

* Sales by Customer Segment
* Sales by Product Category
* Regional Sales Performance
* Customer Segment Comparison
* Product Category Comparison
* Customer, Order, and Sales KPIs

---

### 🔹 3. Order & Profit Analysis

This dashboard focuses on profitability and operational performance.

Key metrics and visualizations include:

* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Discount
* Average Delivery Time
* Orders by Discount
* Orders by Product Category
* Orders by Shipping Method
* Delivery Performance Analysis

---

## 🧮 DAX Measures

Several DAX measures were created to calculate important business metrics, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity Sold
* Total Orders
* AOV
* AIPO
* Total Customers
* ARPC
* Total Discount Given
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Average Discount
* Average Delivery Time

These measures enable dynamic analysis across different filters, categories, customer segments, and regions.
